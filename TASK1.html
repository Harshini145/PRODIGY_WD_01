The process

From cassette to contact sheet

01

Load

Your roll is loaded onto a reel in total darkness, by feel alone.

02

Develop

Chemistry is mixed fresh and held at temperature to the tenth of a degree.

03

Fix & wash

The image is fixed permanent, then washed until every trace of chemistry is gone.

04

Scan

Negatives are dried, sleeved, and scanned at a resolution worth printing.
